## ML FEATURE 1: SYMPTOM-TO-SPECIALTY RECOMMENDATION

Purpose:
Help users navigate to a potentially relevant medical specialty based on selected general symptoms.

Requirements:
- Allow users to select symptoms from a controlled list.
- Use a Decision Tree or Random Forest model.
- Return a suggested medical specialty.
- Explain the recommendation in simple language.
- Provide a button to browse doctors in that specialty.

Important safety requirements:
- This feature is not a medical diagnosis.
- Do not recommend medicines or treatments.
- Do not claim that a specialty recommendation confirms a disease.
- Display a clear medical disclaimer.
- Display appropriate emergency guidance for potentially urgent symptoms.
- Encourage users to consult qualified healthcare professionals.

## ML FEATURE 2: SMART DOCTOR RECOMMENDATION

Purpose:
Recommend suitable doctors based on user preferences.

Consider:
- Selected specialization.
- Doctor availability.
- Preferred appointment time.
- Consultation fee, if available.
- Doctor experience.
- User preferences.

Use content-based filtering or a similarity-ranking approach.

Display:
- Recommended doctor.
- Specialization.
- Availability.
- Explanation of why the doctor was recommended.

Do not invent ratings or qualifications.

## ML FEATURE 3: APPOINTMENT NO-SHOW PREDICTION

Purpose:
Estimate the likelihood that an appointment may be missed.

Consider available appointment-related features:
- Day of the week.
- Appointment time.
- Booking lead time.
- Reminder status.
- Previous attendance information, where lawfully available.

Use a suitable classification model.

Display:
- Low, medium or high estimated risk.
- Simple explanation.
- Suggested reminder actions.

Important:
- Do not use predictions to deny or restrict appointments.
- Do not penalize patients.
- Clearly label predictions as estimates.
- Use fictional or synthetic training data for the demonstration.
- Do not claim real-world predictive accuracy without proper validation.
